# Product vision

Dung Lair Platform is the guild’s public home and a focused recruitment workspace connected to Discord. It also provides a practical foundation for learning full-stack development, backend design, and self-hosted operations.

## Outcomes

1. A visitor understands who the guild is and how to join.
2. An applicant submits once and can find the current status without chasing an officer.
3. Officers review the same application record, see its history, and keep internal discussion private.
4. Discord points officers to new work while the platform remains the authoritative application record.

## Principles

- Deliver a useful guild site before building the broader guild platform.
- Keep domain modules cohesive and communicate through explicit interfaces.
- Separate internal identity and authorization from Discord identity.
- Keep plans, decisions, diagrams, issues, and source together in GitHub.
- Add infrastructure when it solves a demonstrated problem.

## Success evidence

MVP success is demonstrated by a real applicant completing the end-to-end journey, an officer completing a review, and a deployment that can recover its data. Numerical adoption targets have not been agreed; establish them after launch rather than inventing them now.

The implementation is for one guild. Multi-tenant hosting, commercial subscriptions, and multi-guild administration are outside the agreed scope.
